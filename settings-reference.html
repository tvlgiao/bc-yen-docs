    Yen Theme — Settings Reference¶

    Every setting the Theme Editor exposes, grouped the way the editor groups them. 367 settings across 9 sections.

    The i18n.* sections hold storefront wording. They are listed for completeness, but a store that sells in one language rarely needs to touch them — BigCommerce fills them from the theme's language files.

    Section Settings
    Yen Theme 76
    i18n.Global 44
    i18n.HeaderAndFooter 34
    i18n.HomePage 3
    i18n.Products 43
    i18n.ButtonsAndIcons 25
    i18n.CheckoutPage 68
    i18n.PaymentButtons 33
    Lookbook 41
